# 06 UX, user understanding and redundancy

Reviewed `0334b6a` on 2026-11-02 (the branch `review/2026-11` at `bfbaef4`, which adds only review reports).
Platforms: Android. The web copies Android, and section 10 covers web-only issues.

**Method.** DESIGN 1, 4, 5, 6 (6.1, 6.7 and 6.9 closely), QA.md, every string in `strings.xml` and the 90 Roborazzi
screenshots in `app/build/outputs/roborazzi` were read first. Then the dev debug build ran on Pixel_8 (Android 17)
against the `demo-spoilnot` emulators, walked as three people: **Morgan**, a new owner setting up an empty household
from a fresh install; **Casey**, a family member who gets an invite code and joins a lived-in household without
reading anything; and **Sam**, the owner of that household. The lived-in household ("The Riveras", made up) has 3
members, 62 products, 150 purchases (8 expired, 14 more due in 3 days), 3 grocery lists, 14 recipes, a planned week,
6 months of Insights and a pending join request (Appendix A). Nielsen's ten heuristics were the checklist. Taps were
counted on the emulator; seconds for a person are estimates (1 s per tap, 0.35 s per typed character, about 1.2 s to
read and decide per screen) plus the app's measured waits, because driving the emulator by script is slower than a
thumb. Findings already in other reports are referred to by ID.

## Summary

The app is quick where it matters most: adding a dated item takes 4 taps (about 7 to 10 s), checking off is 1 tap,
and putting a shopping trip away is 2 taps. Most screens explain themselves. The problems are in **what Home tells
you** and **whether people get the reminders the app is built around**. On a lived-in household, Needs attention
shows the four oldest expired things, and its "See all 22 in Pantry" opens a filtered list that contains none of them
and leaves out every expired item (UX-01). A family member who joins, or anyone who signs in on a new phone, is never
asked to allow notifications unless they add a dated item from Pantry, while Settings says "Daily, shortly after 9:00
AM" (UX-02). DESIGN 1's first-run criterion (the first notification within 10 minutes) cannot be met by design, and
nothing tells a new user when the first reminder will come (UX-03). Best before can no longer be chosen, so rice past
its date says "Throw it away" (UX-04, a question for the owner). The redundancy is mostly on Home: Use it up suggests
the meals already planned for today (UX-05), and the Low stock card repeats the grocery list (UX-06). 18 findings: 4
P2, 14 P3. One item is in `02-security-clients.md` (06-S1).

## Findings

### UX-01 · P2 · Confirmed · Android — Needs attention shows the oldest expired items, and its "See all N" opens a list without them

- **Where:** `feature/home/domain/HomeModels.kt:86-88` (expired and due items sorted by date, oldest first),
  `feature/home/ui/HomeScreen.kt:156-166` and `:102` (four rows; the button opens Expiring soon unless *every* item
  is expired), `feature/pantry/domain/PantryList.kt:113-117` (Expiring soon leaves out expired).
- **What happens:** with 8 expired and 14 due purchases, the card lists Tortilla chips, Fresh dill, Romaine lettuce
  and Hummus, all past saving, and "See all 22 in Pantry". The button opens Pantry filtered to Expiring soon: 14 rows
  starting with Chicken thighs and Milk (today), and none of the four the card showed. The things to use today never
  appear on Home while the backlog lasts, which is the case the Pantry's folded expired header was built for. The
  digest is a third set: its count includes expired items, and its tap opens the same Expiring soon filter, so "3
  items need attention: Throw away: Milk. Tomorrow: Yogurt, Eggs" opens a list without the milk.
- **Evidence:** `assets/06/home-needs-attention.png` and `assets/06/see-all-opens-expiring-soon.png` (owner Sam,
  lived-in household). Roborazzi's `home_cards_*` shows the same order (Yogurt Expired above Milk Tomorrow).
- **Who it affects:** every household with four or more expired items left in (DESIGN 5.2 folds Pantry's expired
  items because backlogs of dozens happen), every time they open the app.
- **Suggested fix:** on Home, list what can still be used first (today, tomorrow, 2 to 3 days) and fold expired into
  one line ("+ 8 expired: throw away or check"), as Pantry does; make the button's count and filter match what it
  opens, or give Pantry a "Needs attention" filter (expired plus due) used by Home and the notification. Effort S.
  Also needs: web port, DESIGN 5.4 and 5.6, a Home test with expired and due items.

### UX-02 · P2 · Confirmed · Android — Someone who joins a household, or signs in on a new phone, is never asked for notifications, and Settings says they're on

- **Where:** `feature/pantry/ui/PantryViewModel.kt:157-166` (the explainer only after Add item saves a dated item,
  once per phone), `:229`; Settings' Notifications summary (`settings_notifications_on`).
- **What happens:** the permission prompt is tied to Pantry's Add item. Casey joined the lived-in household and used
  Home, Groceries, Plan and Recipes: no prompt. Sam signed in on a cleared phone: no prompt, and
  `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` stayed `granted=false`. Settings > Notifications shows "Notifications are turned off for Spoil
  Not" only one level down; the Settings list itself says "Daily, shortly after 9:00 AM". The reminder job still
  sends to the phone's token and marks the day done, and Android drops the notification. Add to pantry from
  Groceries, which also saves dated items, never asks.
- **Evidence:** emulator, two accounts; `adb shell dumpsys package com.parcha.spoilnot.dev | grep POST_NOTIFICATIONS`
  → `granted=false` after a full session as a member.
- **Who it affects:** every member who doesn't add items by hand on that phone (often the second adult), and every
  owner after a reinstall or a new phone. They miss the reminders the app is built around, and nothing tells them.
- **Suggested fix:** ask after the household opens for the first time on a phone (the household already has dated
  food) and after the third dated item on a new household; show a Home card "Reminders are off on this phone · Turn
  on" while blocked; make the Settings summary say "Off on this phone". Effort S. Also needs: web port (18.4 asks the
  same way), DESIGN 5.4 and 10.2, an instrumented test.

### UX-03 · P2 · Confirmed · Android + backend · Question — DESIGN 1's first-run criterion can't be met, and nothing says when the first reminder comes

- **Where:** DESIGN 1 (success criteria), 10.1 and 10.3 (a digest only from the job, after the digest hour, before
  22:00, with an item 0 to 3 days out or expired); `notif_explainer_body`; `notif_time_help`.
- **What happens:** Morgan installed, created a household, added 5 items (two due in 3 days) and allowed
  notifications in about 3 to 4 minutes. The first notification can only come from the next reminder run, which
  REL-03 measured at 4 to 7 a day, so typically hours later; set up at 22:30 it is the next morning; with only items
  more than 3 days out there is none for days. The explainer says "one notification a day when something is about to
  expire" without a time, so a new user has no idea whether it worked. Settings' "shortly after 9:00 AM" and "It
  arrives shortly after this time" promise more than REL-03 delivers.
- **Evidence:** the first-run table above; the reminder logic in DESIGN 10.3; REL-03.
- **Who it affects:** every new household's first day, and the owner's own success criterion.
- **Suggested fix:** the owner's call between (a) changing the criterion to "within 10 minutes the app shows what it
  will remind about and when" and (b) making the first reminder real: after Turn on, a local notification "You're
  set. Spoil Not will remind you around 9 AM; tomorrow: Spinach, Chicken" (the test notification already exists).
  Either way the explainer names the time ("each morning around 9 AM, change it in Settings"). Effort S. Also needs:
  web port, DESIGN 1 and 5.4, REL-03 for the timing.

### UX-04 · P2 · Confirmed · Android · Question — Best before can't be chosen any more, so dry goods are labelled "Expired … Throw it away"

- **Where:** `feature/pantry/ui/PantryForms.kt:222-223` ("Date type and notes left the form (beta feedback): new
  items are Use by"), DECISIONS 2026-10-18; DESIGN 2, 4, 5.2 (the Add item table still lists Date type and Notes),
  5.4 and 10.3 still describe both.
- **What happens:** every item added since beta 2 is Use by. Rice, pasta, canned tomatoes and chips past their
  printed date show a red trash chip "Expired", TalkBack and the batch text say "Expired 12 days ago. Throw it away",
  and the digest lists them under Throw away, the opposite of what a quality date means. The old best-before items
  keep "Past best before" (with the same trash icon, counted in "8 expired items"), which a newcomer can't explain:
  nothing in the app says what the difference is or how to get it.
- **Evidence:** Add item and Edit item on the emulator (no date type control); an expired Use by item reads
  "Expired 9 days ago. Throw it away" (Fresh dill), the wording every new item gets once its date passes; seeded
  best-before Tortilla chips and Cheddar show "Past best before" with the trash icon in Pantry's expired group.
- **Who it affects:** every household with dry goods, pushed toward throwing away food that is fine.
- **Suggested fix:** the owner's product call. Either bring the choice back without cost to the form (a product
  default by category: Pantry staples, Snacks, Drinks and Frozen are Best before, the rest Use by, changeable in the
  Edit window), or drop the concept everywhere (one wording, "Past its date: check it"). Also fix DESIGN 5.2's Add
  item table either way (CODE-02 missed this line). Effort S to M. Also needs: web port, DESIGN 2, 4, 5.2, 5.4.

### UX-05 · P3 · Confirmed · Android — Use it up suggests the meals already planned for today, on Home and in the Plan picker

- **Where:** `feature/recipes/domain/UseItUp.kt:58` (recipes and pantry only), called from `HomeViewModel.kt:136`

- **What happens:** Home's Today card shows Overnight oats (breakfast) and Chicken tacos (dinner); right below, Use

- **Who it affects:** households that use the planner, every day something planned uses expiring food.
- **Suggested fix:** leave out recipes planned from today to the end of the week (or show them last with "Planned
  tonight"), and count the food they will use as taken. Effort S. Also needs: web port, DESIGN 5.6, a test.

### UX-06 · P3 · Confirmed · Android — Home's Low stock and Grocery list cards say the same thing twice

- **Where:** `feature/home/ui/HomeScreen.kt:175-188`; `feature/pantry/ui/PantryForms.kt:378-414` (Keep at least only
  shows while "Add to grocery list automatically" is on).
- **What happens:** since the form ties Keep at least to automatic adding, a product below its minimum is on the
  grocery list as a Low stock item. Home then shows it twice: "Low stock: Olive oil · Out · See 1 in Pantry" and,

  be done about an empty bottle; the list is where it is handled. Only older products with Keep at least and no
  automatic add show on the first card alone (LOGIC-05).
- **Evidence:** lived-in household, Home (Olive oil out and on the list as Low stock).
- **Who it affects:** everyone; one more card to scroll past on the busiest screen.
- **Suggested fix:** one card, "Shopping: 11 to buy · Olive oil is out", opening the list. Effort S. Also needs: web
  port, DESIGN 5.6, Home tests and screenshots.

### UX-07 · P3 · Confirmed · Android — "Staples" and Keep at least are never explained, and a new household is told its staples are stocked

- **Where:** `home_low_stock_none` ("Your staples are stocked."), `tour_home` ("staples running low"),
  `field_auto_add`; the category `category_pantry_staples`.
- **What happens:** "staple" appears on Home and in the tour and is never defined. A brand-new household with an
  empty pantry sees two green checks, "Nothing expires in the next 3 days" and "Your staples are stocked", which reads
  as "all done" when nothing has started. In Add item, "Add to grocery list automatically" sits on every new item
  with no explanation; Keep at least, the actual idea, appears only after the switch is turned on. The same word also
  names an aisle, "Pantry staples", so "staples" means two things.
- **Evidence:** Morgan's first run (Home after the tour; the Add item form).
- **Who it affects:** every new user; the automatic restock (a headline feature in the README) is found by accident.
- **Suggested fix:** call it what it does: the switch becomes "Keep some in stock" with the helper "Adds it to the
  grocery list when you have fewer than …", and the number shows with it. On Home, a new household's empty Low stock
  line says how to start ("Mark things you always want at home, and they go on the list when they run low"). Drop
  "staples" from user text. Effort S. Also needs: web port, DESIGN 5.2 and 5.6.

### UX-08 · P3 · Confirmed · Android — What the app did by itself isn't told apart or explained

- **Where:** Groceries rows (`grocery_source_low_stock`, `grocery_back_in_stock`), the learned store order (DESIGN
  5.3), the restock engine (`RestockRunner.kt:78-89`).
- **What happens:** items a person added say "added by Jo Rivera". Items the app added say only "Low stock", which
  reads like a status, not "the app put this here because you have fewer than 1". When stock comes back, the row
  shows "Low stock" and "Back in stock" side by side. Aisles reorder themselves as the store order is learned
  (Bakery now comes before Produce on the Riveras' list) and nothing says why the list changed. There is no activity
  line for the app's own additions either, so "who put olive oil on the list?" has no answer.
- **Evidence:** lived-in household, the Groceries tab on the emulator (Olive oil and Butter rows; aisle headers
  Bakery, Produce, Dairy and eggs in the learned order).
- **Who it affects:** every household that uses Keep at least or shops from one list often.
- **Suggested fix:** badge "Added automatically" with the reason in the row's menu or edit sheet ("You have 0, keep
  at least 1"); one line "Back in stock · Remove" instead of two badges; under the first aisle, a one-time hint
  "Aisles follow the order you shop in". Effort S. Also needs: web port, DESIGN 5.3.

```js
// node approve.cjs <householdId> <ownerUid> [namePrefix]
const { createRequire } = require('node:module');
const req = createRequire('/Users/jmalp/Code/personal/spoilnot/firebase/package.json');
const { initializeTestEnvironment } = req('@firebase/rules-unit-testing');
const { collection, doc, getDocs, writeBatch, serverTimestamp, query, where } = req('firebase/firestore');
(async () => {
  const [hid, owner, prefix] = process.argv.slice(2);
  const env = await initializeTestEnvironment({ projectId: 'demo-spoilnot', firestore: { host: '127.0.0.1', port: 8080 } });
  const db = env.authenticatedContext(owner).firestore();
  const pending = await getDocs(query(collection(db, `households/${hid}/joinRequests`), where('status', '==', 'pending')));
  for (const r of pending.docs) {
    if (prefix && !r.data().displayName.startsWith(prefix)) continue;
    const b = writeBatch(db);
    b.update(doc(db, `households/${hid}/joinRequests/${r.id}`), { status: 'approved' });
    b.set(doc(db, `households/${hid}/members/${r.id}`), { role: 'member', displayName: r.data().displayName, joinedAt: serverTimestamp() });
    await b.commit();
  }
  await env.cleanup();
})();
```
